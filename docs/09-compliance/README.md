## Governing sources (to cite at implementation)
- Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), its IRR, NPC circulars/advisories (breach notification, registration, data sharing, DPO).
- Labor Code of the Philippines (PD 442) and DOLE department orders/advisories.
- PD 851 (13th month pay), RA 10653 (13th month tax exemption cap), TRAIN (RA 10963) and later BIR regulations (RR/RMC).
- SSS Law (RA 11199), National Health Insurance / UHC (RA 7875 as amended, RA 11223), Pag-IBIG (RA 9679), related circulars.
- Maternity (RA 11210), Paternity (RA 8187), Solo Parents (RA 8972; RA 11861), VAWC (RA 9262), Magna Carta of Women (RA 9710), OSH (RA 11058), Anti-Sexual Harassment (RA 7877) and Safe Spaces (RA 11313), Telecommuting (RA 11165). **VERIFY each applicability**.
- E-Commerce Act (RA 8792) for e-signatures and electronic records.

## Compliance process
1. Maintain matrix with owner and last-verified date.
2. Each requirement maps to FR/BR and a test or evidence artifact.
3. Quarterly review; immediate review on new circulars.
4. DPO signs the privacy items; counsel signs the legal-interpretation items.
5. Customer responsibilities documented (what the system supports vs. what the employer must decide).

## Shared responsibility
